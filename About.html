Neurofoundry is a small team of engineers and researchers building practical AI systems for people doing real work. We ship local-first software, custom assistants, and free utilities.

We started because the tools we wanted didn't exist — at least not in the form we needed. So we built them. Then someone asked if they could use one. So we shipped it. Now we do that for a living.

§ 02 · MANIFESTO

What we believe, in writing.

AI tooling has a discipline problem. Everything ships as a chatbot wrapped around a black box, sold to people who don't know what they're buying, and abandoned when the next funding round happens.

We think there's a better way. Smaller, sharper tools. Local execution. Transparent state. Support windows measured in years, not press cycles. AI that integrates into work people already do, instead of asking them to do their work differently.

The engine is a mind for the network you design. — from the inscription on our anvil

That's the line we put on the wall, and the question we ask before every commit: does this tool make the person using it more capable, or does it make them more dependent? We only ship the first kind.

§ 03 · PRINCIPLES

Four rules we don't break.

last revised feb 2026
PRINCIPLE 01

Tools first, products second.

Every system we ship started as an internal tool. If we wouldn't reach for it at our own bench, we don't release it.

PRINCIPLE 02

Local by default.

Your data, your machine, your keys. The cloud is a feature, not a foundation. Sovereignty over convenience.

PRINCIPLE 03

Show the seams.

No magic. Every system exposes its inputs, its state, and its reasoning. Black boxes are accidents waiting to ship.

PRINCIPLE 04

Ship slow, support forever.

Fewer releases, longer support windows. We patch tools five years old because someone still depends on them.
