§ 05 · COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you write, maybe we already answered.

6 entries · last revised feb 2026
01 How fast do you respond? +
Most inquiries get a reply within 2–4 hours during business days (Pacific time). Commissions and security reports are triaged within 24h.
02 Do you take small projects? +
Yes. We have a "single tool" engagement track — fixed scope, fixed price, 3–6 weeks. Mention "small commission" in your message and we'll send the brief.
03 What stacks do you work in? +
Python, TypeScript, Rust, Go. We avoid hosted services where local execution is feasible. We will tell you upfront when something needs a cloud component.
04 Is everything you ship open-source? +
Most utilities are MIT. Custom client work belongs to the client. The flagship engines (Thalamus, Aegis) are source-available with a commercial license.
05 Can we meet in person? +
We have a small workshop in Oakland. Visits by appointment for active or prospective clients. Coffee provided, hex motifs on the wall.
06 Do you sponsor or speak? +
Occasionally. Send the brief — venue, date, audience, topic — to [email protected]. We say yes about a quarter of the time.
