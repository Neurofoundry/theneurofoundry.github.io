Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 2026

Neurofoundry ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your data. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have when using our platform.

The Bottom Line: We build tools for individual autonomy and structural integrity. We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We only collect what is strictly necessary to run, secure, and improve the Neurofoundry infrastructure.

Our core work includes designing and operating high-performance systems that connect software agents, model APIs, and business workflows. To keep these systems reliable, secure, and measurable, we process account identifiers, session metadata, interaction logs, and operational telemetry. We prioritize access controls and least-privilege design across all automated and human-in-the-loop operations.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Data: Name, email address, and authentication provider details.
  • Profile Data: Information you voluntarily provide (e.g., avatar, preferences).
  • Technical & Telemetry Data: Logs, device/browser metadata, IP address, and system interactions used for security and debugging.
  • Cookies & Session Data: Essential tokens required for authentication and platform security.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, secure, and optimize the Neurofoundry platform.
  • To authenticate users and manage payment/subscription processing.
  • To send strictly transactional communications (e.g., security notices, verification, billing alerts).
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent abuse, fraud, and policy violations.

3. Third-Party Integrations & OAuth

If you authenticate through third parties (like Google or GitHub), we receive basic profile information governed by your settings with those providers. We recommend reviewing their respective privacy policies.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We never sell your personal data. We only share information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With trusted vendors required to operate our platform (e.g., cloud hosting, payment processors, analytics).
  • Legal Compliance: When required by law, subpoena, or legally binding request, particularly under the jurisdiction of Florida and applicable federal laws.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to confidentiality agreements.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as strictly necessary to provide our services, comply with legal and tax obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

6. Security Measures

We employ robust, industry-standard technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. You are responsible for safeguarding your own login credentials.

7. State-Specific Rights (Including Florida & CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, including residents of Florida or California, you may have specific rights regarding your personal information:

  • The right to know what personal data is collected and how it is used.
  • The right to request deletion of your personal data.
  • The right to opt-out of the sale of personal data (Note: We do not sell your data).
  • The right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us directly at the email provided below.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is strictly for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 13 (or applicable local age threshold). If we discover we have inadvertently collected such data, we will delete it immediately.
