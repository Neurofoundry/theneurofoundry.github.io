Data Deletion Policy

Effective Date: May 2026

Neurofoundry provides users with full control over their personal data. This policy outlines the specific steps you can take to request the permanent removal of your information from our platform.

1. How to Request Deletion

Users may request data deletion through the following methods:

  • In-App Settings: Navigate to your Account Settings and select the "Delete Account" option to initiate an automated purge of your data.
  • Email Request: Contact us at support@theneurofoundry.com using the subject line "Data Deletion Request."

2. Verification Requirements

To protect account security, all manual deletion requests must be sent from the email address currently associated with the Neurofoundry account. We may require additional verification before processing the request.

3. Scope of Data Removal

Upon processing a deletion request, Neurofoundry will permanently remove:

  • Account profile information and credentials.
  • User-provided content and associated metadata.
  • Generated outputs and activity history linked to the account.

4. Retention & Timeline

Requests are generally processed within 30 days. Please note that certain data may be retained if necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements. Once the deletion is finalized, the data cannot be recovered.
