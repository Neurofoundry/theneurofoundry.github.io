Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 19, 2026

Neurofoundry ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have when using our website and services.

Our core work includes designing and operating AI-driven automation systems that connect software agents, orchestration layers, model APIs, and business workflows so teams can run complex operations with better speed, consistency, and auditability. In practice, that means we may process account identifiers, session metadata, interaction logs, and operational telemetry to keep these systems reliable, secure, and measurable, while applying access controls, least-privilege design, and human oversight for sensitive decisions. We build these services to support legitimate product, engineering, and operational use cases, and we continuously tune them to reduce abuse risk, improve system integrity, and maintain accountable behavior across automated and human-in-the-loop paths.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information (name, email, authentication provider details).
  • Profile information you provide (avatar, bio, preferences).
  • Usage and technical data (logs, device/browser metadata, IP address).
  • Cookies/session data for authentication and security.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide, secure, and improve the service.
  • Authenticate users and manage accounts.
  • Send transactional communications (verification, password reset, service notices).
  • Investigate abuse, fraud, and policy violations.

3. OAuth and Third-Party Providers

If you sign in through Google or GitHub, we receive basic profile information from those providers according to your settings and their policies.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share data with service providers that help run our platform (hosting, email, analytics, infrastructure), and when required by law.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as needed to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. No method of transmission or storage is guaranteed 100% secure.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, update, delete, or export your data. Contact us to submit a request.

8. Children's Privacy

The service is not directed to children under 13 (or applicable local age threshold), and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
