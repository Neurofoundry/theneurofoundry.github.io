§ 02 MANIFESTO
OUR
WORKING
BELIEF

AI tooling has a discipline problem. We're trying to fix it — one small, durable, transparent tool at a time.

Everything ships as a chatbot wrapped around a black box, sold to people who don't know what they're buying, and abandoned when the next funding round happens. We think there's a better way: smaller, sharper tools. Local execution. Transparent state. Support windows measured in years.

§ 03 · PRINCIPLES

Four rules we don't break.

last revised feb 2026
01

Tools first, products second.

Every system we ship started as an internal tool. If we wouldn't reach for it at our own bench, we don't release it.

02

Local by default.

Your data, your machine, your keys. The cloud is a feature, not a foundation. Sovereignty over convenience.

03

Show the seams.

No magic. Every system exposes its inputs, state, and reasoning. Black boxes are accidents waiting to ship.

04

Ship slow, support forever.

Fewer releases, longer support windows. We patch tools five years old because someone still depends on them.
