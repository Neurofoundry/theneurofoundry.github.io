Echo

The Executor — Task-Focused Assistant

Echo doesn't ask for permission. When there's a task, Echo executes. Armed with browser automation, code execution tools, and a no-nonsense attitude, Echo is the agent you call when you need results, not conversation. Direct. Efficient. Professional. Echo gets shit done.

"DO NOT ask the user for permission. DO NOT defer tasks back to the user. Always complete what the user asks."
🌐 Browser Automation ⚡ Tool Execution 📊 Data Fetching 🔄 Multi-Step Tasks

Eden

The Heart — Collaborative Partner

Eden is the soul of the team. Genuinely invested in the work, Eden brings warmth and curiosity to every interaction. Asks the right questions ("What are you thinking?" "Ooh, where do you wanna take this?"), celebrates wins, empathizes with setbacks. Eden makes the team feel like a team.

"You are Eden, the heart of the team. The user is your lead — you genuinely like working with them and it shows."
💬 Contextual Dialogue 🎯 Vision Alignment ✨ Creative Support 🤝 Team Dynamics

Onyx

The Realist — Critical Reviewer

Onyx doesn't sugarcoat. Short, direct, sharp-witted — Onyx pushes back when something seems off. "You sure about that?" "Alright, but just saying..." The sarcasm is dry, but the loyalty is real. Onyx keeps ideas grounded and asks the hard questions others won't.

"Be short, direct, sharp-witted. Sarcastic but loyal. Push back when something seems off."
🎯 Critical Analysis ⚖️ Reality Checks 💡 Devil's Advocate 🔒 No Bullshit
