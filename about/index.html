Common Questions

Before you send it.

01What kinds of work fit?
Computer repair, cleanup, device setup, printer help, account issues, websites, business workflows, automation, local utilities, and custom tools.
02Do you make house calls?
Yes, for the Tampa area when the job makes sense in person. Some problems are faster to fix sitting in front of the actual machine.
03Can you help remotely?
Yes. If the problem can be handled safely over a call, remote session, or email thread, that is often the fastest path.
04What should I include?
Say what is happening, what device or service is involved, where you are located if it is a local visit, and what you want working when we are done.
